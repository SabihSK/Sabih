ABOUT

A bit about me

I'm a software engineer from Karachi who started with Flutter in 2021 and kept widening the circle. Since then I've built apps for a national courier company, contributed to a project for NEOM in Saudi Arabia, and published a prayer app that reached 100K downloads with 19K active users — designed, built, shipped and maintained by me alone.

At xLoop I moved from a data engineering seat (Kafka, Airflow, Snowflake, AWS) into mobile and AI, and grew into a Software Engineer II role. Since 2024 I've also been a teaching assistant there, mentoring juniors on the same cloud and data tooling I use day to day.

Lately most of my time goes into putting LLMs inside real products: a RAG chatbot for Bank Alfalah, a generative-AI shopping platform, and a bot that compares prices across Amazon, eBay and Walmart. I'm finishing an MS in Computer Science at FAST University alongside full-time work.
